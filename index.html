PhoneX Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

PhoneX collects information necessary to facilitate device valuation, doorstep pickup, and technician inspections:

  • Account Details: Name, phone number, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Device Diagnostics: Hardware specifications, device condition declarations, IMEI/Serial numbers for verification, and technician inspection photos.
  • Location & Address: Pickup and delivery addresses provided for dispatching field technicians.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Calculate automated market valuations for trade-in devices.
  • Dispatch verified field technicians for doorstep evaluation and pickup.
  • Process order lifecycles and direct payments to customer accounts.
  • Prevent fraud and ensure compliance with mobile device trade-in regulations.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is only accessible to authorized operational personnel and assigned field technicians to fulfill service requests.

PhoneX Terms and Conditions

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Services Overview

PhoneX operates a mobile phone trade-in, buyback, repair, and resale platform connecting customers with field technicians and certified device processing facilities.

2. Device Ownership & Legal Title

By submitting a device through PhoneX, you declare and warrant that:

  • You are the sole legal owner of the device or have explicit authorization from the owner to sell/exchange it.
  • The device is free from all liens, claims, financing locks, stolen reports, or unauthorized third-party claims.
  • Legal title and ownership of the device transfer permanently to PhoneX once the physical inspection is accepted and payout is completed.

3. Valuations & Physical Inspections

Initial quotes generated in the app are preliminary estimates based on user-submitted condition answers. The final payout offer is determined by the on-site physical inspection performed by an assigned PhoneX technician. If the actual condition differs from the declared answers, PhoneX reserves the right to issue a revised final quote. Customers maintain full rights to accept or decline the final offer prior to payout.

4. User Data Removal Prior to Handover

Customers are strictly responsible for backing up personal data and performing a complete factory reset (removing iCloud, Google Accounts, and biometric locks) before handing the device to the technician. PhoneX is not liable for data loss or personal data remaining on handed-over devices.

5. Payouts & Cancellations

Payouts are processed via approved electronic transfer methods once the customer confirms acceptance of the technician's verified offer. Either party may cancel the pickup request at any point before payment completion and device handover.
