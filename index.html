PhoneX Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

PhoneX collects information necessary to facilitate device valuation, pickup, and technician inspections:

  • Account details (name, email address, phone number).
  • Device diagnostics, condition answers, IMEI/Serial number for trade-in verification, and inspection photo evidence.
  • Pickup and delivery addresses.

2. How We Use Information

We use this data solely to calculate device resale quotes, dispatch field technicians, process customer payouts, and manage order lifecycles.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell user personal data. Information is only shared with assigned field technicians and verified operational partners to complete requested services.

Account and Data Deletion Request

Users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

To submit a deletion request, email us at: support@phonex.local (or your support email) with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email address. All account records and personal data will be purged within 30 days.
